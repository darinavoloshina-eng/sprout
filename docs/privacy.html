Privacy Policy

Last updated September 10, 2026

GardenWise is a garden-planning app built to work entirely on your phone. We don't run servers that store your data, we don't have user accounts, and we don't have a way to see your garden even if we wanted to. This page explains exactly what the app does with the few pieces of information it touches.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter into GardenWise: your crops, planting dates, watering setup, harvest log, task history, and any photos you add, is saved locally on your phone using standard on-device storage. None of it is uploaded anywhere. If you delete the app, or use "Delete account" in Settings, that data is gone for good. There is no copy anywhere else to restore it from.

Location

If you share your location or type in an address, GardenWise uses those coordinates for one purpose: to look up your local weather and estimate your area's frost dates, so planting and watering guidance is timed to where you actually garden instead of a generic month or hardiness zone.

To do this, your coordinates (or the address you typed) are sent directly from your phone to the following services, with no GardenWise server in between:

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com)
    Current and historical weather data for your coordinates.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org)
    Turns a typed address into coordinates, when you search for one manually.
  • BigDataCloud (bigdatacloud.net)
    Turns coordinates into a readable place name, when you use "Use my location."

Each of these lookups sends only the coordinates or text needed to answer that one request, nothing that identifies you. GardenWise does not store your location on a server, and none of these services receive any other information about you or your garden. Each has its own privacy policy governing how it handles that request.

Email

If you enter an email address during setup or in Settings, it's stored only on your device. We don't send anything to it: no digest, no marketing, and it's never transmitted anywhere.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they're scheduled directly on your phone through iOS. No notification server is involved, and no data leaves your device to make this work.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts or logins
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No analytics or behavioral tracking software
  • We never sell your data, to anyone, for any reason

Affiliate links (upcoming)

A future version of GardenWise may include links to third-party retailers, such as Amazon, for products related to your garden (a fertilizer a task mentions, for example). If you tap one of those links and make a purchase, GardenWise may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Clicking a link like this takes you to that retailer's own website, governed by their own privacy policy, not this one. GardenWise does not send the retailer any of your personal data. The link itself is the only thing that connects the referral.

Children's privacy

GardenWise isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone in that age group. Since the app doesn't collect personal information from any user in the first place, this holds true regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Since GardenWise doesn't collect contact information from most users, we have no way to notify you directly, so please check back here occasionally.
