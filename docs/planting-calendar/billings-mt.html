Common questions

When is the last frost in Billings, MT?

Around May 10 on average, based on the last ten years of daily temperature records. Any given year can run a week or two either way, so keep an eye on the forecast before planting frost-tender crops.

When is the first frost in Billings, MT?

Around October 11 on average. Cover or harvest tender crops like tomatoes, peppers and basil before then.

When should I plant tomatoes in Billings, MT?

Start tomato seeds indoors around March 29 and plant them outside around May 17, after the last frost, when nights stay above about 50°F.

Can I grow a fall garden in Billings, MT?

Yes. Cool-season crops do well planted in late summer and fall: cauliflower around July 12, broccoli around July 19, arugula around July 26 and cabbage around July 26.

How these dates are worked out: the last and first frost are averages over the last ten years of the days the temperature near Billings dropped to 34°F or below, from Open-Meteo's historical weather records. We count 34°F rather than 32°F because frost forms in the coldest spots of a garden before the wider area reads freezing.
