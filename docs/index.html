Why this exists

Hardiness zones don't really tell you much.

The usual way

Zone 7b covers coastal Oregon and the Blue Ridge Mountains.

Most planting advice just gives you a month, or a zone map. That means the same tips for a foggy coastal patio and a sunny valley forty miles away, because a zone was never really built to know the difference.

What GardenWise does

Your exact location, not a rough guess.

GardenWise looks up real, historical frost dates for the spot you actually garden in. And if that data looks off (say, a mild coastal spot pushing a fall frost date months later than it should be), GardenWise catches it and falls back to something sensible instead of quietly giving you bad advice.

What it actually tracks

Gentle nudges that keep you organized and your plants thriving.

🔔

A nudge, right when it matters

GardenWise isn't just a checklist you have to manage yourself. It keeps an eye on your plants' actual stage and today's weather, then tells you exactly what to do: water now, plant more arugula, feed the lemon tree. A daily reminder means nothing sneaks up on you.

🌱

Replant reminders, but only when they matter

Arugula grows fast and bolts within two weeks, so GardenWise reminds you to plant more before you run out. Carrots get a reminder every three weeks. Peas don't get one at all, since they stop growing well once summer heat hits, and no frost date can predict that.

🍋

Fertilizer tips made for each plant

Citrus trees like to be fed every month while they're growing. Figs barely need any food at all; feed one too much and you'll get lots of leaves but no fruit. GardenWise knows the difference for every plant, instead of giving you one generic reminder for everything.

🧺

A harvest log that remembers last year

Every harvest you log adds up to a real recap of your season: how much you picked, your longest streak, which crop came in earlier than last year. Once you've logged two seasons, Pro compares them automatically.
