Replant reminders, but only when they matter

Arugula grows fast and bolts within two weeks, so GardenWise reminds you to plant more before you run out. Carrots get a reminder every three weeks. Peas skip it, since summer heat ends their season anyway.

🍋

Fertilizer tips made for each plant

Citrus trees like to be fed every month while they're growing. Figs barely need any food at all; feed one too much and you'll get lots of leaves but no fruit. GardenWise knows the difference for every plant, instead of giving you one generic reminder for everything.

A harvest log that remembers last year

Every harvest you log adds up to a real recap of your season: how much you picked, your longest streak, which crop came in earlier than last year. Once you've logged two seasons, GardenWise Pro compares them automatically.
