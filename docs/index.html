Help along the way

Simple care that helps your plants give you more food.

A nudge when your plants need you

GardenWise isn't just a checklist you have to manage yourself. It keeps an eye on your plants' actual stage and the weather, then tells you exactly what to do: start the peppers indoors, sow more arugula, feed the lemon tree. It only pings you on days there's a real job, so your plants stay healthy and productive without you having to keep track. When it rains, it skips the watering.

More to harvest, all season long

Arugula grows fast and bolts within two weeks, so GardenWise reminds you to plant more before you run out. Carrots get a reminder every three weeks. Peas skip it, since summer heat ends their season anyway.

Feeding that grows more food

Tomatoes want regular feeding once they start fruiting. Figs barely need any; feed one too much and you'll get lots of leaves but no fruit. GardenWise knows the difference for every plant, and points you to organic options like compost and fish emulsion.

See how much food you grew

Every harvest you log adds up to a real recap of your season: how much you picked, what it would cost at the store, and which crop came in earlier than last year. Once you've logged two seasons, GardenWise compares them automatically.

Questions

Good to know before you grow.

What is GardenWise?

GardenWise is an iPhone app that helps you grow your own food. It tells you what grows well where you live, when to start seeds, when to plant, and when to water, feed and harvest, all timed to the real frost history at your exact location.

Is GardenWise good for beginners?

Yes. It's made for people who have never grown anything. It uses plain language, gives you one clear next step at a time, and includes Seed Starting 101, a five-step guide to starting seeds indoors.

Do I have to start seeds indoors?

No. Many crops, like beans, peas, carrots and radishes, are sown straight into the garden, and GardenWise tells you which ones. For crops that do better with a head start, like tomatoes and peppers, it gives you start-indoors, harden-off and transplant dates and walks you through it in Seed Starting 101. Buying seedlings from a nursery instead? Skip the indoor steps with one tap.

How does GardenWise know when I should plant?

It looks up the last ten years of daily temperature records for your location to estimate your last spring frost and first fall frost. Then it counts forward or back for each crop, so your dates fit your backyard instead of a broad hardiness zone.

Is GardenWise free?

Yes. GardenWise is free for up to four crops, with every feature included: planting and seed-starting dates, the Seed Starting 101 guide, a watering plan, and reminders to feed, protect and harvest. GardenWise Pro removes the crop limit for $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year, each with a 14-day free trial, or $59.99 once for lifetime access.

What can I grow with GardenWise?

68 crops: vegetables, herbs, berries and fruit trees. That includes tomatoes, peppers, lettuce, kale, carrots, garlic, basil, strawberries, blueberries, lemons, figs and apples.

Do I need a big garden?

Not at all. One 4 × 4 ft raised bed is plenty to start, and four crops (the free plan) is a great first garden. Tell GardenWise your bed size and sun, and it sizes your watering plan to match. Citrus and figs grow well in large pots too.

Does GardenWise work where I live?

GardenWise works from your exact location, whether your winters are cold, mild or somewhere in between. In mild-winter areas it times cool-season crops like lettuce and broccoli for fall and winter, when they grow best.

Is GardenWise on Android?

Not yet. GardenWise is available for iPhone on the App Store.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up. Your garden is saved on your phone, and an email address is optional.
