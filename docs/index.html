Help along the way

Simple care that helps your plants give you more food.

A nudge when your plants need you

GardenWise isn't just a checklist you have to manage yourself. It keeps an eye on your plants' actual stage and the weather, then tells you exactly what to do: start the peppers indoors, sow more arugula, feed the lemon tree. It only pings you on days there's a real job, so your plants stay healthy and productive without you having to keep track. Watering comes as one short plan on Sunday, not a buzz every morning.

🌱

More to harvest, all season long

Arugula grows fast and bolts within two weeks, so GardenWise reminds you to plant more before you run out. Carrots get a reminder every three weeks. Peas skip it, since summer heat ends their season anyway.

🍋

Feeding that grows more food

Tomatoes want regular feeding once they start fruiting. Figs barely need any; feed one too much and you'll get lots of leaves but no fruit. GardenWise knows the difference for every plant, and points you to organic options like compost and fish emulsion.
