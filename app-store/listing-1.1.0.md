## Description (2382/4000)

Grow your own food. Start with a seed. End with a meal.

GardenWise is the garden planner for anyone who wants to grow their own vegetables, herbs, and fruit, even if you've never grown anything before. Tell it where you live and what you'd like to grow. It tells you what to plant, when to start seeds, and what to do each week, all timed to the real frost history in your backyard, not a generic zone map.

KNOW WHAT TO GROW
• Suggestions for what grows well where you live, sorted by when to plant
• 68 crops: vegetables, herbs, berries, and fruit trees, from tomatoes and kale to lemons and figs
• Spring and fall planting windows worked out from your local frost dates

START WITH A SEED
• Start-indoors, harden-off, and transplant dates for every crop
• Seed Starting 101: what you need and how to do it, in five short steps
• Buying seedlings instead? Skip the indoor steps with one tap

GROW MORE FOOD
• A watering plan for your bed size, sun, and drip or hand watering, adjusted for rain
• Feeding reminders for each crop, with an organic fertilizer suggestion
• Frost alerts that name exactly which plants to cover tonight
• Diagnose a plant: a quick step-by-step check when something looks wrong, and what to do about it

END WITH A MEAL
• Harvest alerts so you pick at the right time
• Log every harvest and watch your season add up
• Photos and notes for each plant, to look back on next year

REMINDERS THAT DON'T NAG
• A notification only on days there's a real job to do
• Nothing due? No notification

FREE AND PRO
GardenWise is free for up to four crops, with suggestions for what to grow, a watering plan, real frost-date planting windows, and seed-starting dates and guide.

GardenWise Pro unlocks every crop (including apples, citrus, figs, olives, and avocado), grow-more tips for feeding, pruning, and picking, reminders for every job, frost and harvest alerts, and year-over-year comparison. Pro is available monthly or yearly, each starting with a 14-day free trial, or as a one-time lifetime purchase.

After the free trial, subscriptions are charged to your Apple ID account and renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings.

## App Review Information: notes (update before submitting)

Thanks for reviewing GardenWise.

No account or login is needed. On first launch, pick a few crops, then set a location: "Use my location" asks for When In Use permission, or type a city name or ZIP code instead. Both paths work, and the app is fully usable without granting location.

Pro: tap the "Try Pro free for 14 days" banner in Settings (or any PRO-tagged feature) to reach the purchase screen. It offers a monthly and a yearly subscription and a one-time Lifetime purchase, plus Restore.

Email: email is optional. "Email updates" and "Weekly garden email" in Settings are separate opt-ins.

Shopping links: the seed-starting guide, feeding tasks, plant pages, Diagnose results, and bird-netting reminders have optional "Buy"/"Shop" buttons that open a product page on Amazon in the browser. These are Amazon Associates affiliate links (tag gardenwise-20), disclosed in the app next to the links. Nothing is sold or purchased inside the app, and no digital content or features are unlocked through them.

Notifications are local only (no push server).
