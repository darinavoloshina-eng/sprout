Grow your own food, with a plan made for your exact backyard.

GardenWise tells you what to plant, when to start seeds, and when to feed and harvest, all timed to the real frost history where you live. Not a generic zone map. Then it only pings you when there's a real job to do.

KNOW WHEN TO START SEEDS
• Start-indoors, harden-off, and transplant dates for every crop, worked out from your last frost
• A built-in Seed Starting 101 guide: what you need and how to do it, in five short steps
• Buying seedlings instead? Skip the indoor steps with one tap

GROW WITH CONFIDENCE
• Planting windows for vegetables, herbs, and fruit, for your exact location
• Feeding reminders tailored to each crop, with organic options
• Frost alerts that name exactly which plants to cover tonight
• Diagnose a plant: a quick step-by-step check when something looks wrong

REMINDERS THAT DON'T NAG
• A notification only on days there's a real job: sowing, feeding, planting, transplanting
• Watering comes as one short plan on Sunday evening, not a buzz every morning

YOUR SEASON, REMEMBERED
• Log every harvest and watch your season add up
• Photos and notes for each plant, to look back on next year

FREE AND PRO
GardenWise is free for up to four crops, with a live watering schedule, real frost-date planting windows, and seed-starting dates and guide.

GardenWise Pro unlocks every crop (including citrus, figs, olives, and avocado), reminders for every job, frost and harvest alerts, the season view, and year-over-year comparison. Pro is available monthly or yearly, each starting with a 14-day free trial, or as a one-time lifetime purchase.

After the free trial, subscriptions are charged to your Apple ID account and renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings.
