## Description (2116/4000)

GardenWise tells you exactly what your garden needs and when, based on the real frost history for your exact location, not a generic zone map.

Most planting advice gives you a month or a hardiness zone. But two gardens in the same zone can be weeks apart in spring. GardenWise looks up the frost history for where you actually garden and times everything to it.

KNOW WHEN TO START SEEDS
• Start-indoors, harden-off, and transplant dates for every crop, worked out from your last frost
• A built-in Seed Starting 101 guide: what to buy and how to do it, in five short steps
• Buying seedlings instead? Skip the indoor steps with one tap

REMINDERS THAT DON'T NAG
• A notification only on days there's a real job: sowing, feeding, planting, transplanting
• Watering comes as one short plan on Sunday evening, not a buzz every morning
• Nothing due? No notification

CARE FOR EVERY PLANT
• A live watering schedule for your bed size, sun, and drip or hand watering, adjusted for recent and upcoming rain
• Feeding and replanting reminders tailored to each crop: citrus wants monthly feeding, figs barely any
• Frost alerts that name exactly which plants to cover tonight
• Diagnose a plant: a quick step-by-step check when something looks wrong

YOUR SEASON, REMEMBERED
• Log every harvest and see your season add up
• A calendar of everything coming up
• Photos and notes for each plant, to look back on next year

FREE AND PRO
GardenWise is free for up to four crops, with a live watering schedule, real frost-date planting windows, and seed-starting dates and guide.

GardenWise Pro unlocks all 70 crops (including citrus, figs, olives, and avocado), reminders for every job, frost and harvest alerts, the season view, and year-over-year comparison. Pro is available monthly, yearly, or as a one-time lifetime purchase.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings.

Thanks for reviewing GardenWise.

No account or login is needed. On first launch, pick a few crops, then set a location: "Use my location" asks for When In Use permission, or type a city name instead. Both paths work, and the app is fully usable without granting location.

Pro: tap the "Try Pro free for 14 days" banner in Settings (or any PRO-tagged feature) to reach the purchase screen. It offers a monthly and a yearly subscription and a one-time Lifetime purchase, plus Restore.

Email: email is optional. "Email updates" and "Weekly garden email" in Settings are separate opt-ins. We're collecting sign-ups ahead of launch, so turning on the weekly email shows a "You're on the list" message; nothing is sent from inside the app.

Shopping links in the seed-starting guide and on feeding tasks open plain Amazon search results in the browser. They are not affiliate links and nothing is purchased in the app.

Notifications are local only (no push server).
