# GardenWise app wording: "Grow your own food" review (DRAFT)

**Core idea:** anyone can grow their own food, even if they know nothing, and GardenWise shows them how. Reminders exist to help plants grow healthier and give more food.

**Voice:** encouraging, plain words, never intimidating. Lead with food and confidence. Never "tasks" for their own sake.

Nothing here is changed in the app yet. Mark up anything you'd like different.

## Flowers
- Remove the **Flowers** category and its 7 crops (marigold, zinnia, cosmos, pansy, nasturtium, sunflower, dahlias) from setup and "Add a crop".
- Anyone who already added a flower keeps it; they just can't add new ones.

## New: "What grows well near you right now"
- **Where:** a button on setup step 1, and a row at the top of "Add a crop".
- **Shows:** up to three short lists: *Start indoors now*, *Plant outside now*, *Coming up in a few weeks*. Each crop has a one-line reason ("Ready in about 8 weeks · loves cool weather") and a **+ Add** button.
- **Picks crops that:** can be planted or started now or soon at the user's location, fit between their last and first frost, and suit their winters (no citrus where it freezes). Beginner-friendly crops first.
